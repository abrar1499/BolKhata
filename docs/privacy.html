Smart Expense (BolKhata) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Your data stays with you

Smart Expense is a personal khata and expense app. Your entries, accounts and chat are saved on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we do not collect, sell or share your entries or messages.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app saves a copy of your data in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so you can get it back on another phone. Only this app can open that folder; we cannot see it. Sign out at any time from the side menu.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID and basic information about the device and the app (see how Google uses information from apps). Ads never get your entries or messages. Where the law asks for it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it later. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your phone's settings.

App improvements

The app downloads a small public file of phrase patterns (for example how a sentence like "name ko number diye" is read), so it understands more without a new version. Nothing about you is sent to get it. If you use the optional AI assistant, the phrase patterns the app learns from it are added to that public file, with names and numbers taken out first; a pattern that still holds a name or a number is not sent.

Other languages

For a language the app does not read itself, your message is turned into English on your phone (Google ML Kit). Your words are not sent anywhere for this; only the language's pack (about 30 MB) is downloaded from Google once.

AI assistant (optional, off by default)

The app reads your messages on your phone. If you add your own key for an AI service (for example Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, Mistral or OpenRouter) and turn it on, what you type or say in the chat, with the account names and entries needed to answer, is sent to that service to be understood, under that service's privacy policy. Your key stays on your phone and is not part of any backup. Turn the key off at any time and nothing more is sent.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you tap the mic. Speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service (for example Google's), under that service's privacy policy; the app itself keeps no recording.
  • Internet: for the optional Google Drive backup, ads, the app-improvement file and the optional AI assistant above.

Receipts and PDF statements

Receipts and bank statements you import are read on your phone (offline text recognition). They are not uploaded.

Backups and deleting data

A backup file is one you create and keep yourself. To delete all your data, clear the app's data or uninstall it; to remove the Drive copy, delete the app's data in Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps) or sign out first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
