Smart Expense (BolKhata) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Your data stays on your phone

Smart Expense is a personal khata and expense app. Your entries, parties, khatas and chat history are saved only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

AI help (Groq)

To understand what you type or say, the app uses Groq (groq.com), an AI service. It is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Groq AI → Turn off; the app then works fully offline.

  • What is sent: each chat message you send, your voice recordings when you use the mic, the last few lines of the chat, and the names of people in your khatas (so names are spelled right).
  • What is not sent: your saved entries and balances. Totals and reports are worked out on your phone.
  • Why: only to read your message or speech and reply. We do not sell it or use it for ads. Groq processes it for us; Groq's own privacy policy applies.

If you add your own Groq key in Settings, it is used instead and stays only on your phone (left out of backups).

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you tap the mic, to hear what you say.
  • Notification access (optional): if you allow it, the app reads bank alerts on your phone to suggest entries. These alerts are read on the device and are never uploaded.
  • Internet: only for the AI help above.

Receipts and PDF statements

Receipts and bank statements you import are read on your phone (offline text recognition). They are not uploaded.

Backups and deleting data

A backup is a file that you create and keep yourself. To delete all your data, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
