Smart Expense (BolKhata) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Your data stays on your phone

Smart Expense is a personal khata and expense app. Your entries, parties, khatas and chat history are saved only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

Optional online features

These are off until you turn them on and enter your own key:

  • Groq voice and chat help. If you add a Groq API key, your voice recordings and chat messages are sent to Groq (groq.com) to turn speech into text and fix spelling. The names of your khatas may be sent with them to improve spelling. Groq's own privacy policy applies.
  • ElevenLabs voice. If you add an ElevenLabs key, spoken replies and voice calls go through ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io). ElevenLabs' own privacy policy applies.
  • Chat review. If you enter your own GitHub token and repository, your chat log is saved to that repository so you can review it. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Your keys and tokens are stored only on your phone and are left out of backups.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you tap the mic, to hear what you say.
  • Notification access (optional): if you allow it, the app reads bank alerts on your phone to suggest entries. These alerts are read on the device and are never uploaded.
  • Internet: only for the optional features above.

Receipts and PDF statements

Receipts and bank statements you import are read on your phone (offline text recognition). They are not uploaded.

Backups and deleting data

A backup is a file that you create and keep yourself. To delete all your data, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
