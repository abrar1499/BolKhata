# Smart Expense — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Smart Expense is a personal khata / expense app. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores
- The entries you add (amounts, dates, notes, names of people or shops you type) are saved **only on your phone**.
- Backups you export are files you choose to save or share yourself.

## What the app collects or shares
- The app does **not** collect, sell or share your personal data with us or with any third party.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no account sign-in.
- The microphone is used only while you tap the mic to speak an entry. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service; the app does not store the audio.

## Permissions
- **Microphone**: only to turn your speech into an entry, when you choose to speak.
- **Internet**: only if you turn on an optional online feature yourself; your entries stay on the phone.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything it stored.

## Children
The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: write to the developer at the email shown on the app's Google Play page.
